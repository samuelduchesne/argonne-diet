How this works
The Argonne Anti-Jet-Lag Diet sets an anchor at your destination’s breakfast time on the day you arrive. Count back four days and alternate FEAST → FAST → FEAST → FAST. On arrival morning, break the fast at destination breakfast with a high-protein meal. Caffeine: days 1–3 only between 15:00–17:00; day 4 is morning if traveling west, or 18:00–23:00 if traveling east. No alcohol on the plane.
